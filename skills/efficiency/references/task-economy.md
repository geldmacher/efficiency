# Task Economy

Use this reference only when the request assesses the effort, context, or output of a task. It guides choices within the existing assignment and grants no additional execution or editing authority.

Use Pareto prioritization: focus effort on the steps that contribute most to the agreed goal or reduce material risk. Treat "80/20" as a heuristic, not a measured ratio, effort budget, or completion threshold. Preserve all agreed requirements, required checks, and necessary safeguards, including rare failures with severe consequences. Scale the assessment to the task; a small, clear fix needs no separate prioritization exercise.

Infer the phase from the request and the available evidence:

- **Before work:** assess likely cost drivers and output, then prioritize steps by expected benefit, effort, material risk, and dependencies. Preserve prerequisite work and recommend proportional validation and actions that deserve explicit attention.
- **During work:** identify avoidable context reads, command noise, redundant narration, unnecessary documentation or artifacts, speculative abstractions, delegation, retries, tool calls, or validation effort. Judge additional work by its contribution to the agreed goal; recommend stopping optional refinement that would not materially improve the outcome.
- **After work:** assess whether resources and generated output were proportional, identify what materially improved the result, a decision, or necessary verification, and report material under-spending risks or concrete improvements. Treat work as complete only when all agreed requirements and required checks are satisfied and material risks have adequate verification. Keep missing necessary evidence explicit.

Report low value only for a concrete item whose removal or reduction improves the result, a decision, or necessary verification. Explain the evidence and the alternative. Length, tone, or an AI-written appearance alone are not findings; preserve material evidence and uncertainty.

When RTK data informs the assessment, keep execution coverage, shell-output reduction, contributor concentration, and whole-task net effect distinct, and report the evidence scope and host attribution. Do not present cumulative RTK gain as savings from one task or invent monetary cost without user-supplied rates.
